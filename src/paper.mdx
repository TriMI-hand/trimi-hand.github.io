## Abstract

TriMI is a tri-modal manipulation interface for direct demonstration, joint-level haptic teleoperation, and robotic execution. Its four-finger design provides 13 degrees of freedom: four in the thumb and three in each of the remaining fingers. With a bill of materials cost of approximately USD 800, excluding the tracking device and wrist camera, TriMI enables operators to collect demonstrations through direct physical interaction, teleoperate robotic hands with joint-level haptic feedback, and execute learned manipulation policies. We evaluated a diffusion policy trained on the collected direct demonstrations, achieving 18 successful trials out of 20 for cube pick-and-place (90%) and 12 out of 16 for cap unscrewing (75%). A full open-source release is coming soon.
